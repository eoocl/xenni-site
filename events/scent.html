The Art of Scent

冬序闻香

An intimate exploration of Chinese incense traditions, scent, and ritual, with Lin-school floral art and a guqin performance at the table.

一场以东方香文化为主题的冬日雅集。从香材、香器到闻香体验，并有花道与古琴展示。

I

Materials香材

Where scent comes from, and how to tell one material from another: woods, resins, seeds and petals.

认识香的来处，分辨不同香材的气味。

Also at the Table

Flowers & the Guqin花道与古琴展示

Lin-school Floral Art林派花道

A live arrangement for the winter season, in the Lin-school tradition our members studied in Suzhou: how a stem is chosen, cut and placed.

以齐间成员在苏州研习的林派花道，现场为冬日插一器花。

Guangling-school Guqin广陵琴派古琴

A guqin performance between the incense and the conversation, from the Guangling school of Yangzhou.

香事与交谈之间，一曲广陵琴派古琴。

This December, step into a world of timeless craftsmanship, delicate fragrances, and meaningful encounters.

The Art of Scent is the quiet first table of our Winter Series. The second, Velvet Blooms & Winter Incense on December 24, is about making things together.

「冬序闻香」偏静，是感官与文化的体验；「绒花映冬」偏动，是亲手创作与相识。
