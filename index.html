Suzhou · Lin-school floral art · 苏州林派华道
01

Go into the Field田野调查

We don’t stop at the table. We travel to where a craft is still alive and learn from the people who hold it: Lin-school floral art in Suzhou, Li-style taiji at Tianzhen Garden. Members hear about each trip and can come with us.

我们不只办雅集，也走进田野：去苏州学林派华道，去天真园访李氏太极，跟着传承人学、记录、做课题。社群成员第一时间收到田野招募，可以一起出发。

First to Know, First to Sit优先入席

Our tables are small and by application. Group members hear about each gathering first and reserve directly.

About XENNI关于齐间

A youth-founded cultural venture incubated at Peking University. We learn intangible heritage in the field, from the people who still hold it, and turn it into salons, programmes, objects and writing for young people growing up between cultures.

齐间是一支由青年发起、在北京大学孵化的文化创业团队。以非遗为切入口，面向在国际语境中成长的青少年与留学生，跟着真正的传承人，用身体与感受去学习一门文化。
