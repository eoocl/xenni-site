—— PROLOGUE ——

Song brocade — one of China's three great brocades. Quiet patterns, restrained color: an aesthetic woven straight into silk.

宋锦起于宋，中国三大名锦之一。纹样温润，设色清雅，是织进丝线里的东方审美。

This Sunday,
we make it into a fan.

这个周日，我们把它做成一柄扇

Flowers, tea, and sweets made for the season — an afternoon to slow all the way down. One gathering, and the start of a summer.

一席花，一道茶，一份应季茶点。把整个下午慢下来。一场雅集，一个夏天的开端。

I
THE FAN
宋 锦 团 扇

Choose the brocade, the color, the form — and shape a round fan with your own hands. Guided from start to finish, no experience needed. Finish it, take it home.

择锦、配色、成形，亲手制一柄团扇。老师全程带，零基础亦可，做完带走。

宋 锦 · Song brocade
II
THE FLOWERS
一 席 花

Arrange a single vessel of seasonal blooms. Negative space, asymmetry — the heart of Eastern floral art. Hands and eyes, quietly settling.

随时令插一器花。留白、不对称，东方花道的讲究。一枝一叶之间，慢下来。

花 道 · Eastern floral art
III
THE TEA
一 道 茶

一道茶，一份节气茶点。杯盏之间，聊手艺，聊季节，聊这个夏天。

IV
THE SUMMER AHEAD
从一个下午到一个夏天

Elegance is the doorway. Once you've made something and sat with it, the real questions surface — how does a craft like this get seen today? Understood across cultures?

雅致是入口。做过，坐过，问题自然浮现——这样一门手艺，如何在今天被看见？如何被世界读懂？

This Sunday, students from Peking University and overseas institutions sit down with you — to talk about what a craft like this can mean for young people today. Read through anthropology, design and communication, the same object carries different meaning in different systems of knowledge. The work we call 非遗 ICAP began with exactly these questions.

这个周日，来自北京大学与海外院校的学长学姐，会和你坐下来聊聊——一门手艺，对当代青年意味着什么。从人类学、设计学、传播学切入，同一件东西，在不同知识体系里有着不同的意义。我们做非遗 ICAP，也正是从这些问题开始的。

Some questions don't close in an afternoon.

有些问题，不会在一个下午里结束

ABOUT
About XENNI
关 于 齐 间

XENNI is a youth-founded cultural venture incubated at Peking University. Starting from intangible heritage, we design immersive cultural learning and camps for young people growing up in international contexts — letting them learn culture through the body and the senses, in real cultural fieldwork.

齐间，是一支由青年发起、在北京大学孵化的文化创业团队。我们以非遗文化为切入口，面向在国际语境中成长的青少年与留学生群体，设计沉浸式的文化学习与营会——让他们用身体、用感受，去做一场真正的文化田野学习。

China holds one of the richest systems of intangible heritage and craft in the world, yet young people learn far too little of it. We work to bring these traditions the young people willing to come close — and to give those young people a cultural path that takes root and lasts a lifetime.

我们相信，中国拥有全世界最丰富的非遗与工艺体系，但年轻人学的还太少。我们想做的，是为这些珍贵的传承找到愿意走近它的年轻人，也为年轻人找到一条能扎根、能受益一生的文化路径。

Our wish is simple, and large —
that young people, in China and the world,
can find the roots of their lives.

我们的愿望很简单，也很大——
让中国青年、世界青年，都能找到他们生命的根。
